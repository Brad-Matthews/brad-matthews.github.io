Brad Matthews

Fintech product engineer · consumer financial platforms

I design and ship financial products end to end — the bank data pipelines, the money logic, the AI layer, and the compliance scaffolding that lets a financial institution say yes.

My work sits where consumer finance meets applied AI: bank data aggregation and transaction enrichment, recurring-payment and subscription detection, cash-flow forecasting, and conversational financial guidance grounded in governed, auditable sources rather than model guesswork. I build the whole stack, because in fintech the hard parts live in the seams — between the aggregator and the ledger, between the model and the disclosure.

AI-assisted development lets me cover ground that used to require a team, and I stay deliberately current — rebuilding my toolchain as the models and tooling shift rather than settling into last year's stack. Speed is the point, but only if what ships survives review.

So I hold my own work to the standard a bank's reviewers would apply: single-authority architecture, additive migrations behind flags, fail-closed error handling, tenant isolation, PII tokenization, provenance on every fact an AI surfaces, and certification evidence for every release. Governance from the first commit, not retrofitted for a security questionnaire.
